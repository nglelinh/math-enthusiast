The **AdS/CFT correspondence**—also called **gauge/gravity duality** or **holography** in this setting—is the flagship modern physical duality: a theory of gravity (or strings) in a bulk spacetime with negative cosmological constant is dual to a quantum field theory *without gravity* living on the lower-dimensional conformal boundary. Proposed by **Juan Maldacena (1997)** and developed by Witten, Gubser–Klebanov–Polyakov, and many others, it is the deepest known bulk ↔ boundary dictionary in theoretical physics.

This deep dive expands [Duality as a principle]({{ site.baseurl }}/contents/en/chapter06/06_12_Duality_Principle/). **Status honesty first:** treat AdS/CFT as a **research program with extraordinarily well-tested examples**, not as a single finished theorem that closes quantum gravity.

- Explain why **AdS** geometry naturally has a **conformal boundary**.
- Describe the **dictionary** idea: operators ↔ bulk fields; correlators ↔ bulk computations; large $$N$$ ↔ classical gravity.
- Explain **computational leverage**: strong coupling on one side ↔ weak/classical on the other.
- Distinguish holography as a duality principle from ordinary symmetry of one Lagrangian.
- Avoid overclaims (AdS ≠ cosmology of our universe; not every QFT has a known dual).

**Prerequisites.** Duality principle; mathematical-physics map. GR and QFT appear at slogan level only.

## 1. What problem does holography address?

Quantum gravity is hard in the bulk. Quantum field theory on a fixed background is comparatively well developed. Holography proposes that, in special settings, the two are **equivalent descriptions**: every bulk gravitational question has a dual QFT answer on the boundary, and vice versa. The **holographic principle** (’t Hooft, Susskind, black-hole thermodynamics) suggested degrees of freedom scale with area, not volume; AdS/CFT is the sharpest known realization.

---

## 2. AdS geometry in one page

**Anti–de Sitter space** $$\mathrm{AdS}_{d+1}$$ is the maximally symmetric spacetime with **negative** cosmological constant. Roughly, it is a Lorentzian analogue of hyperbolic space. Crucially, it has a **conformal boundary** at infinity: light rays reach the boundary in finite coordinate time in convenient charts, and the boundary inherits a conformal class of metrics.

That boundary is the natural home of a **conformal field theory (CFT)**—a quantum field theory invariant under conformal transformations (including scale invariance). The bulk radial direction often dualizes to **energy scale** (RG flow intuition): deep interior ↔ infrared; near boundary ↔ ultraviolet.

---

## 3. The canonical pair

Maldacena’s original conjecture (slogan form):

> Type IIB string theory on $$\mathrm{AdS}_5\times S^5$$ (with $$N$$ units of five-form flux) is dual to four-dimensional $$\mathcal N=4$$ super Yang–Mills theory with gauge group $$\mathrm{SU}(N)$$.

| Bulk (gravity / strings) | Boundary (QFT) |
|--------------------------|----------------|
| $$\mathrm{AdS}_5\times S^5$$ | 4d CFT: $$\mathcal N=4$$ SYM |
| String coupling / $$N$$ | Gauge coupling / rank $$N$$ |
| Classical supergravity limit | Large $$N$$, strong ’t Hooft coupling window |
| Bulk fields | Gauge-invariant operators |

Related dualities (ABJM, other spheres, orbifolds, …) form a web. The **MAGOO review** (Aharony–Gubser–Maldacena–Ooguri–Oz) is the standard map of the early landscape.

---

## 4. The dictionary (what “dual” means operationally)

A duality is useless without a **map of observables**. AdS/CFT supplies one:

1. **Operators ↔ fields.** Each gauge-invariant local operator on the boundary corresponds to a bulk field (or string mode) with matching quantum numbers.  
2. **Correlators ↔ bulk propagators.** $$n$$-point functions of boundary operators equal bulk amplitudes with sources fixed by boundary conditions near $$\partial\mathrm{AdS}$$.  
3. **Large $$N$$ ↔ classical gravity.** When $$N$$ is large and the ’t Hooft coupling is large, bulk string theory often reduces to classical supergravity—computable geometry answers hard QFT questions.  
4. **Geometry ↔ states.** Bulk geometries (including black holes) correspond to states or ensembles in the CFT; horizon thermodynamics dualizes to thermal CFT.

**GKPW formula** (Gubser–Klebanov–Polyakov / Witten) packages the generating functional of boundary correlators as a bulk gravitational path integral with fixed boundary values—the practical engine of many calculations.

---

## 5. Why dualities buy leverage

| Hard on one side | Easier on the dual |
|------------------|--------------------|
| Strongly coupled gauge theory | Classical / semiclassical gravity |
| Real-time thermal physics | Black-hole geometry in AdS |
| Entanglement structure | Minimal surfaces in bulk (Ryu–Takayanagi culture) |

This is the same **strong ↔ weak** spirit as S-duality, but bulk ↔ boundary rather than electric ↔ magnetic inside one dimension count.

## 6. Status and cautions (2026)

| Claim | Label |
|-------|--------|
| Canonical supersymmetric examples and large-$$N$$ dictionaries | Physics standard; immense evidence |
| Full nonperturbative equivalence in all regimes | Program / conjecture strength varies by formulation |
| Mathematical theorem for complete string/QFT pair | Not a single closed theorem covering all cases |
| Condensed-matter “holographic models” | Often *inspired by* AdS/CFT, not derived duals of real materials |
| Our cosmological universe | Closer to **de Sitter**; dS/CFT is harder and less settled |

**Caution.** Popular accounts sometimes say “string theory solved quantum gravity.” More accurate: AdS/CFT is a controlled laboratory where quantum gravity questions can be *translated* into QFT questions (and vice versa) in special spacetimes.

## 8. Confusions

| Claim | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| “AdS/CFT is fully proved like Poincaré duality.” | Research correspondence with controlled examples. |
| “Boundary theory includes dynamical gravity.” | Standard AdS/CFT: boundary is nongravitational QFT. |
| “Applies directly to flat-space LHC physics.” | Flat-space limits and non-AdS duals are research; canonical setting is AdS. |
| “Holography = any bulk/boundary analogy.” | AdS/CFT is a precise dictionary class; metaphors are not dualities. |
| “Black holes in AdS are only classical GR.” | Dual CFT describes quantum microstates/thermodynamics. |

### C1. Better name: holography / gauge–gravity duality

Maldacena emphasizes that “AdS/CFT” is a narrow label for a broader idea: a **quantum gravity theory in a bulk spacetime** is dual to a **quantum field theory on the boundary** of that spacetime. Many examples involve string theory as a way to quantize gravity; the correspondence is a dictionary between two complete theories, not a single Lagrangian symmetry.

### C2. Basics first, supersymmetry later

Part 1 aims at the **basic idea without supersymmetry or integrability**. Later lectures / schools add tools. For this course: hold the slogan and the dictionary culture; do not require $$\mathcal N=4$$ mastery to state the correspondence.

### C3. Computational leverage

In a regime of **weakly coupled strings** (semiclassical bulk gravity), the dual **gauge theory is typically strongly coupled**. That is the same moral as S-duality: hard on one side becomes easier on the other. Boundary **operators** and **correlation functions** match bulk fields / bulk computations (details in later parts and in MAGOO / TASI written sources).
