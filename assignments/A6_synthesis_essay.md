# A6 — Synthesis essay: personal map + critique

| | |
|--|--|
| **Weight** | 15% |
| **Due** | Week 15 |
| **Length** | 1500–2000 words |
| **Outcomes** | LO1, LO3, LO4, LO6 |
| **AI tier** | **O** — AI collaboration allowed; graded on judgment beyond the tool; **process note required** |

## Purpose

Capstone: show a **personal map** of modern mathematics from this course and a disciplined **critique** of popular exposition—synthesis, not a new research paper.

## Task

Write an essay with **all** of the following:

### A. Personal map (≈ half)

- Choose **three nodes** from different course sections (e.g. RH, Kakeya/dimension, crypto, infinity, Euclid/Cantor, AI math, Collatz studio).  
- For each node: one precise statement + why you care + how it connects to another node.  
- Include at least one **open problem** (LO1) and one **proof idea** (LO3) among the three (can be the same node serving dual roles if explicit).

### B. Application mechanism (LO4)

- In one subsection, give a **one-step mechanism** linking a classical field to a named technology (crypto flagship is the default).

### C. Critique (LO6)

- Take **one** popular or secondary source (news, YouTube transcript, popular book chapter, blog).  
- Quote or paraphrase one claim.  
- Explain how it **overstates, understates, or confuses** the mathematics.  
- Rewrite the claim in two precise sentences.

### D. Process note (required)

- Tools used (if any).  
- What you verified against course flagships or primary sources.  
- What you changed after checking.  
- One thing the tool got wrong or flattened.

## Rubric

| Criterion | Excellent | Satisfactory | Needs work |
|-----------|-----------|--------------|------------|
| **Map & connections** | Three sharp nodes; real links | Nodes present; weak links | List without synthesis |
| **LO1 / LO3** | Precise open problem + usable proof idea | Partial | Missing or wrong |
| **LO4 mechanism** | Correct one-step mechanism | Vague but not false | Buzzword only |
| **LO6 critique** | Concrete overclaim + rewrite | Mild critique | No engagement with precision |
| **Process note (Tier O)** | Specific verification story | Generic | Missing |
| **Writing** | Coherent whole | OK | Fragmented |

## What not to do

- Recycle A3 or A4 unchanged without new synthesis.
- Critique tone only (“this article is dumb”) without mathematical precision.
- Submit chatbot text with an empty process note.
