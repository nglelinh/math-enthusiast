## Purpose

Seminars work only if people prepare and talk carefully. A1 rewards **prepared engagement** and one **discussion leadership** turn—not volume of speech or agreement with the instructor.

## Task

1. **Weekly participation.** Arrive having read the flagship; bring one precise question or a place of confusion. Contribute to discussion (questions, clarifications, critiques of slogans, board work).
2. **Discussion leadership (once).** On your assigned week you will:
   - Open with a **2–3 minute** framing: theorem/problem in plain language + one stake.
   - Pose **two discussion questions** (not yes/no).
   - Help the group stay on **statement vs slogan** and **idea vs technical detail**.

Sign-up sheet: [NEEDS PROFESSOR INPUT: LMS / in-class sheet].

## Criteria (holistic)

| Level | Participation | Leadership turn |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Excellent** | Consistent preparation; advances discussion with precise language; cites the reading | Clear framing; strong questions; manages time and inclusion |
| **Satisfactory** | Usually prepared; relevant contributions most weeks | Adequate framing and questions |
| **Needs work** | Frequent unpreparedness; rare or off-topic contributions | Thin framing; no usable questions |
| **Unsatisfactory** | Chronic absence/unpreparedness | Missed leadership without makeup plan |

Absences: more than two unexcused → meet instructor (see syllabus). Leadership week absence requires advance notice and reschedule when possible.

## What not to do

- Dominate airtime or derail into unshared technical dumps.
- Use devices to generate live talking points (Tier P).
- Treat popular YouTube metaphors as substitutes for the flagship statement.
