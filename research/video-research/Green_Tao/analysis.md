## 1. Topic status

Green–Tao theorem **proved** (2004 preprint; Annals 2008). Twin primes and bounded gaps are separate stories (Zhang/Maynard).

## 2. Key definitions

### Arithmetic progression of length $$k$$

$$a, a+d, \ldots, a+(k-1)d$$ with $$d>0$$.

### Szemerédi’s theorem

Any positive upper-density subset of $$\mathbb{Z}$$ contains APs of arbitrary length.

### Pseudorandom majorant

Weight $$\nu$$ dominating primes and behaving like a random set for Gowers norms.


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)

- Green–Tao: primes contain infinitely many APs of every length $$k$$.

## 4. Common confusions

| Claim | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| “Green–Tao proves twin primes.” | No — AP length can be large while gaps grow; bounded gaps is GPY/Zhang/Maynard. |
| “Primes have positive density.” | Prime density is zero; the proof uses a relative Szemerédi theorem. |
