## 2. Key definitions

### Riemann zeta (series region)

For $$\operatorname{Re}(s)>1$$,

$$
\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}n^{-s}=\prod_{p\ \mathrm{prime}}(1-p^{-s})^{-1}.
$$

### Analytic continuation

$$\zeta$$ extends meromorphically to $$\mathbb{C}$$ with a single simple pole at $$s=1$$. Functional equation relates $$\zeta(s)$$ and $$\zeta(1-s)$$ (via Gamma / completed $$\xi$$).

### Trivial vs non-trivial zeros

- **Trivial zeros:** $$s=-2,-4,-6,\ldots$$ (from Gamma factor).
- **Non-trivial zeros:** zeros in the critical strip $$0<\operatorname{Re}(s)<1$$.

### Riemann Hypothesis (open)

Every non-trivial zero satisfies $$\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$$ (the **critical line**).

## 3. Status and partial results (slogans)

| Claim | Status as of 2026 |
|-------|-------------------|
| Full RH for $$\zeta$$ | **Open** (Millennium) |
| Prime number theorem | **Proved** (1896); equivalent to no zeros with $$\operatorname{Re}(s)=1$$ |
| Positive proportion of zeros on the line (Hardy–Littlewood / Selberg / Levinson / Conrey lineage) | **Proved** partial; not 100% |
| RH for zeta of curves over finite fields (Weil / Deligne) | **Proved** analogue |
| Numerical checks of many zeros on the line | **Evidence**, not proof |

**Extraction slogans for essays**

1. PNT = main term for $$\pi(x)$$; RH = quality of the **error term** via zeros.  
2. Checking zeros is **verification**, not a proof that all zeros obey RH.  
3. Trivial zeros do **not** violate RH.  
4. GRH is the broader family for Dirichlet $$L$$-functions.

## 4. Common confusions

| Claim | Verdict |
|-------|---------|
| “Billion zeros checked ⇒ RH true” | **False** as proof |
| “RH is a formula for the $$n$$th prime” | **False** |
| “Trivial zeros break RH” | **False** |
| “Zeta is only the series” | Incomplete — RH lives after continuation |
