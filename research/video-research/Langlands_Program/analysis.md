## 1. Topic status

Fundamental Lemma proved (Laumon–Ngô special case; Ngô general). Langlands program remains largely open.

## 2. Key definitions

### Endoscopy

Comparing automorphic data on related reductive groups via transfer of orbital integrals.

### Orbital integral

Integral of a test function over a conjugacy class (or stable conjugacy class) on a $$p$$-adic group.

### Fundamental Lemma

Identity between orbital integrals on a group and stable orbital integrals on endoscopic groups (Langlands–Shelstad transfer factors).

### Hitchin fibration

Geometric map whose fibers' cohomology encodes the orbital-integral identities.


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)

- Laumon–Ngô: Fundamental Lemma for unitary groups (2004 era).
- Ngô: Fundamental Lemma for general reductive groups (Lie algebra form; Publ. Math. IHÉS 2010).

## 4. Common confusions

| Claim | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| “Ngô founded Langlands.” | Langlands proposed the program decades earlier; Ngô unlocked a central comparison lemma. |
| “FL is the whole Langlands program.” | FL is a bottleneck tool for endoscopic comparison of trace formulas, not the full functoriality vision. |
