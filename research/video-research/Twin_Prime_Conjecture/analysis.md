# Mode B — analysis: Twin Prime Conjecture

## Status (2026)

| Statement | Status |
|-----------|--------|
| Infinitely many primes $$p$$ with $$p+2$$ also prime | **Open** |
| $$\liminf (p_{n+1}-p_n) < \infty$$ | **Proved** (Zhang; Maynard; Polymath) |
| Explicit bound $$H=246$$ (unconditional lineage) | **Proved** (community optimization) |
| Gap 2 under Elliott–Halberstam-type hypotheses | Conditional results exist; not full TPC |

## Definitions

Twin primes: pairs $$(p,p+2)$$ both prime.  
Hardy–Littlewood twin prime constant $$C_2$$ and asymptotic conjecture for the count of twins up to $$x$$.

## Extraction slogans

1. Bounded gaps ≠ twin primes.  
2. Parity barrier: sieves struggle to separate primes from almost-primes in a way that forces gap 2.  
3. Zhang opened the door; Maynard multi-dimensional weights simplified and strengthened; Polymath optimized.  
4. Heuristics (divergent $$\sum 1/(\log n)^2$$) suggest infinity; they are not proofs.

## Confusions

| Claim | Verdict |
|-------|---------|
| “Zhang proved twin primes” | **False** |
| “$$H=246$$ almost finishes twins” | Numerically close, methodologically still open |
