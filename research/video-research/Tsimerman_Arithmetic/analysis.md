## 1. Topic status

André–Oort in major cases **proved** via o-minimal / point-counting methods. Broader Zilber–Pink landscape open.

## 2. Key definitions

### André–Oort conjecture

Special points Zariski-dense in a subvariety of a Shimura variety force the subvariety to be special.

### O-minimality

Model-theoretic tameness of real structures (cell decomposition, controlled topology).

### Pila–Wilkie counting

Bounds on rational points of bounded height on transcendental parts of definable sets.

### Period map

Analytic map from a moduli/period domain encoding Hodge data; algebraicity of images is nontrivial.


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)

- Tsimerman (with collaborators / prior Pila–Zannier methods): André–Oort for Siegel modular varieties; Griffiths-type algebraicity of period images (per IMU citation).
