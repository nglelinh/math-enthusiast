## 2. Key definitions

### Concentration

A r.v. $$X$$ concentrates if $$P(|X-\mathbb{E}X|>t)$$ decays rapidly in $$t$$.

### Intrinsic dimension

Effective degrees of freedom of a data set, often $$\ll$$ ambient $$d$$.

## 3. Formal status / theorem (seminar honesty)

**Status:** **Classical + modern probability.** Concentration of measure and JL lemma are theorems; geometric aspects of data analysis remain active.

**Statement / slogan:**

Concentration: Lipschitz functions on high-dimensional spheres/Gaussians are nearly constant. Johnson–Lindenstrauss: $$n$$ points in Euclidean space embed in $$O(\varepsilon^{-2}\log n)$$ dimensions preserving distances up to $$1\pm\varepsilon$$.
