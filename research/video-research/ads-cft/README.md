## Core slogan

Gravity / strings in $$(d+1)$$-dim AdS bulk ↔ CFT without gravity on $$d$$-dim boundary. Canonical: IIB on $$\mathrm{AdS}_5\times S^5$$ ↔ $$\mathcal N=4$$ SYM$$_4$$.
