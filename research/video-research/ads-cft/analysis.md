# AdS/CFT — Mode B analysis

## Status

| Piece | Label |
|-------|--------|
| Original conjecture (Maldacena 1997) | Physics standard; not a single textbook “theorem” for all variants |
| Large-$$N$$ / supersymmetric dictionary examples | Extremely well tested in physics literature |
| Bulk reconstruction / entanglement (Ryu–Takayanagi, etc.) | Active research; partial rigorous math |
| Applications to condensed matter | Often holographic *models*, not derived duals |

## Definitions

- **AdS:** anti–de Sitter spacetime (negative cosmological constant); hyperbolic-like geometry with conformal boundary.
- **CFT:** conformal field theory (scale-invariant QFT with conformal symmetry).
- **Holography:** bulk degrees of freedom encoded on lower-dimensional boundary.
- **Dictionary:** operators ↔ bulk fields; correlators ↔ bulk bulk-boundary propagators; large $$N$$ ↔ classical gravity limit.

## Confusions

- “String theory proved quantum gravity” overclaim.
- AdS ≠ de Sitter cosmology (our universe is closer to dS; dS/CFT harder).
- Not every strongly coupled QFT has a known gravity dual.
