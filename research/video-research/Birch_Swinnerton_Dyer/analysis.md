# Mode B — analysis: Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer

## Status (2026)

**Open** in full. Partial theorems for many curves of analytic rank 0 and 1 (Gross–Zagier, Kolyvagin, Coates–Wiles CM, modularity stage-setting).

## Key objects

- Elliptic curve $$E/\mathbb{Q}$$: nonsingular cubic, group law, point at infinity.
- Mordell–Weil: $$E(\mathbb{Q})\cong \mathbb{Z}^r\oplus E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}}$$ with rank $$r\ge 0$$.
- $$L(E,s)$$: Hasse–Weil $$L$$-function; modularity ⇒ analytic continuation.
- **BSD (rank part):** $$r = \operatorname{ord}_{s=1} L(E,s)$$ (analytic rank = algebraic rank).
- **Refined BSD:** leading Taylor coefficient of $$L$$ at $$s=1$$ equals an arithmetic package (regulator, $$|E(\mathbb{Q})_{\mathrm{tors}}|$$, Sha, Tamagawa, real period).

## Extraction slogans

1. Modularity builds the analytic stage; it does **not** finish BSD.  
2. Rank $$\le 1$$ theory is deep and largely in hand for many curves; higher rank is the open frontier.  
3. ECC over finite fields ≠ Millennium BSD over $$\mathbb{Q}$$ (**LO6**).  
4. Analytic rank 0 predicts no infinite-order rational points (torsion may remain).
