## Core slogan

S-duality of twisted 4d $$\mathcal N=4$$ SYM (compactified on a curve) organizes geometric Langlands: categories of branes / sheaves on moduli of $$G$$-bundles dualize to spectral data for $$G^\vee$$, with mirror symmetry of Hitchin systems as a geometric engine.
