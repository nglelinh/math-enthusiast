## Knowledge units

### Complex numbers as plane points

x²+1=0 has no real root; define i as the vertical unit so pairs (a,b) form C with i²=-1. Each complex number is a vector from origin.

### Multiplication = scale + rotate

Multiply moduli; add arguments. Multiply by i is a 90° rotation (modulus fixed). i⁴=1; i²=-1 is half-turn of 1.

### Where i appears in tech/physics

AC phasors; Fourier as sum of rotating vectors; digital audio/images/Wi‑Fi; quantum wavefunctions; iteration → Mandelbrot-like beauty. FTA: every degree-n polynomial splits in C; nth roots of unity equally spaced on circle.
