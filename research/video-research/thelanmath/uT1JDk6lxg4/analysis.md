### Casus irreducibilis / Bombelli bridge

Cubic with three real roots can force cube roots of complex intermediates in Cardano’s formula; Bombelli (1572) treats them algebraically and recovers real roots—complex as a bridge, not as ‘fake’ answers. Descartes’ pejorative ‘imaginary’ misleads.

### Three centuries to geometry

Wessel–Argand–Gauss make the plane picture precise: numbers as rotations and scalings. Applications: AC/telecom phasors; digital media via Fourier; modern tech stack depends on C.

### Roadmap toward RH-style peaks
