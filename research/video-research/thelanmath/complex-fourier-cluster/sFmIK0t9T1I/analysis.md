### Weak simplex conjecture (coding)

Signal points in R^d for d+1 messages: regular simplex long believed optimal against noise (Shannon-era intuition; weak simplex conjecture named 1988). Strong version false (Steiner 1994). Popular 2026 report: weak form proved via Gaussian max inequalities / correlation comparison (author names LO6). Side door from Fourier lesson into information geometry—not a Fourier theorem.
