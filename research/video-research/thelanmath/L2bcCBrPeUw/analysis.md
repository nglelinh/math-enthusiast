## Knowledge units

### Riemann mapping theorem (slogan)

Any nonempty simply connected proper open subset of C is biholomorphic to the unit disk. Normalization (fix a base point, positive derivative) selects uniqueness. Existence is deep; formula rarely explicit.

### Montel / normal families in the proof culture

Modern proofs use normal families (Montel): locally bounded holomorphic families are normal; extremal stretch-at-center argument among maps into the disk. Course-level: names the toolkit, not full proof.
