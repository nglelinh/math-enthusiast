## Knowledge units

### Real C∞ that is not analytic

Classic bump e^{−1/x²} (extended by 0 at 0) is infinitely differentiable on R but Taylor series at 0 is identically 0—does not recover the function. Real differentiability is weak.

### Holomorphic once ⇒ analytic

Complex differentiability on an open set (holomorphic) implies infinitely differentiable and locally equal to power series (Cauchy integral formula engine). One complex derivative buys infinite smoothness + rigidity.

### Identity / continuation teaser

Analytic continuation / identity theorem: values on a set with limit point determine the function on a connected domain—popular ‘magic’ of complex analysis. LO6: precise hypotheses matter.
