## Knowledge units (reconstructed)

### Needles as skeleton of wave concentration (0–120s)

Frequency cap of angular width δ on sphere ⇒ spatial tube width ~1/δ and length ~1/δ² (scaling as told). Union of multi-directional tubes is Kakeya-type; restriction becomes geometric tube packing.

### Hollow needles: Furstenberg sets (120–200s)

Relax Kakeya in two ways: (1) on each line keep only an s-dimensional dust (not full segment); (2) take only a t-dimensional family of lines (not every direction). Question: minimal Hausdorff dimension of the union.

### Ren–Wang 2023 planar theorem (200–320s)

Every planar Furstenberg set has dim ≥ min(S+T, (3S+T)/2, S+1). Three regions of (S,T)-plane: additive regime, grid/middle, saturation. At S=T=1 recover dim≥2 = planar Kakeya (Davies) as special case. Sharp examples known; not mere lower bound culture.

### Two-ends and Restriction dictionary (320–500s)

Two-ends inequality for line/tube bundles: mass cannot concentrate on a short segment of each line. Plane versions proved (full thickness later); 3D still conjectural. Conditional: two-ends ⇒ full restriction. Exponent↔dimension dictionary: e.g. popular 3.2 ↔ Kakeya 7/3; 22/7 ↔ Wolff 2.5. Solving 3D Kakeya does not automatically set restriction to 3.
