## Knowledge units

### Why SC after Riemann mapping

Riemann mapping guarantees a disk↔region map but rarely writes a formula. Schwarz–Christoffel gives explicit integral maps from half-plane/disk to polygons by prescribing turning angles at prevertices.

### Straight sides + corners as branch factors

Walk straight edges until a vertex, turn by prescribed angle—encoded by power factors in f'. Applications: conformal maps in engineering (airfoils, electrostatics) at slogan level.
