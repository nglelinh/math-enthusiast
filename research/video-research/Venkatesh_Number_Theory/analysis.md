## 2. Key definitions

### Subconvexity

Beating the convexity bound for $$L$$-function growth on critical lines / families.

### Homogeneous dynamics

Flows on quotients of Lie groups (unipotent/diagonal actions) used as a tool for arithmetic equidistribution.

### Equidistribution of arithmetic objects

Arithmetic points become uniformly distributed with respect to natural measures.
