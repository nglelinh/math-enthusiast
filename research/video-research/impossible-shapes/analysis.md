## 2. Key definitions (course-aligned)

### Impossible object

2D drawing suggesting a 3D solid that cannot embed consistently in $$\mathbb{R}^3$$.

### Local vs global

Each vertex/junction ok; global incidence or depth inconsistent.

### Necker ambiguity

Multiple 3D interpretations of one line drawing.
