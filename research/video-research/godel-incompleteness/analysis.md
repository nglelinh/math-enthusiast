## 2. Key definitions

### Formal theory

Language + effective axioms + rules of inference; theorems = derivable sentences.

### Gödel sentence (idea)

Self-referential arithmetical sentence asserting its own unprovability in $$T$$.


---

## 3. Formal status / theorem (seminar honesty)

**Status:** **Proved** (Gödel 1931). First and second incompleteness theorems are theorems of mathematical logic; popularizations often overclaim 'math is broken'.

**Statement / slogan:**

Any consistent, effectively axiomatized theory capable of arithmetic is incomplete: there are true (in $$\mathbb{N}$$) sentences unprovable in the theory. Second: such a theory cannot prove its own consistency (under standard formalizations).
