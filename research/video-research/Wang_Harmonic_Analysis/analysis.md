## 1. Topic status

3D Kakeya conjecture **proved** (Wang–Zahl 2025). Higher-dimensional Kakeya and sharp restriction largely **open**.

## 2. Key definitions

### Kakeya set

A set in $$\mathbb{R}^n$$ containing a unit line segment in every direction.

### Hausdorff dimension

Critical gauge exponent for Hausdorff measure.

### δ-tube

δ-neighborhood of a unit segment; multi-scale packing object.

### Sticky Kakeya

Near-extremal configurations with hierarchical clustering of directions/tubes.


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)

- Wang–Zahl: volume estimates for unions of convex sets → Kakeya set conjecture in three dimensions (arXiv:2502.17655).
- Prior: sticky Kakeya work (arXiv:2210.09581); Furstenberg sets (Ren–Wang).

## 4. Common confusions

| Claim | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| “Besicovitch zero area contradicts Kakeya conjecture.” | Zero measure can still force full Hausdorff dimension; Davies refined plane story. |
| “Solves all dimensions.” | 3D is the breakthrough; higher D open. |
