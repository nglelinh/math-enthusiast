## 2. Key definitions (course-aligned)

### Self-similarity

Structure recurring under scaling (exact or statistical).

### Cantor set

Middle-third removals; uncountable, measure zero, nowhere dense.

### Box/Hausdorff dimension

Scaling exponents capturing intermediate “size.”

### Mandelbrot

Parameters $$c$$ where $$z\mapsto z^2+c$$ orbit of $$0$$ stays bounded.
