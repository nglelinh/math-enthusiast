## 2. Key definitions

### Degree

Number of edge ends at a vertex (loops contribute 2).

### Eulerian circuit / trail

Closed / open walk using each edge exactly once.


---

## 3. Formal status / theorem (seminar honesty)

**Status:** **Proved** (Euler 1736 necessity; full sufficiency of Eulerian criteria completed later, Hierholzer). Founding theorem of graph theory.

**Statement / slogan:**

A connected graph has an Eulerian circuit iff every vertex has even degree; an Eulerian trail iff exactly 0 or 2 odd-degree vertices. Königsberg multigraph has four odd vertices → impossible.
