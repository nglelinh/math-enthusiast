## Core slogan (seminar level)

A nonzero function and its Fourier transform cannot both be sharply concentrated. In quantum mechanics, position and momentum wavefunctions are a Fourier pair, so $$\sigma_x\sigma_p\ge\hbar/2$$. The same Fourier fact has a fractal upgrade: $$f$$ and $$\hat f$$ cannot both live near line-porous fractal sets.
