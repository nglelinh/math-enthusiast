**Goal.** Hear that uncertainty is a Fourier fact, then a quantum fact.

- 3B1B Fourier transform (if needed)
- 3B1B *more general uncertainty principle*

**Goal.** Write Kennard and Robertson; name CCR and Stone–von Neumann as slogans; refuse energy–time and observer-effect mixups.
