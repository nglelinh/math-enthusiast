## 2. Key definitions

### Elliptic curve (slogan)

Smooth cubic curve $$y^2=x^3+Ax+B$$ with group law.

### Frey curve idea

From $$a^n+b^n=c^n$$ form $$y^2=x(x-a^n)(x+b^n)$$ — too exotic to be modular if non-modular cases existed.


---

## 3. Formal status / theorem (seminar honesty)

**Status:** **Proved** (Wiles 1995, with Taylor; modularity for semistable elliptic curves + Ribet). Status closed; full modularity theorem later completed by others.

**Statement / slogan:**

No positive integers $$a,b,c,n$$ with $$n>2$$ satisfy $$a^n+b^n=c^n$$. Modern idea: Frey curve from a hypothetical solution would be a non-modular semistable elliptic curve, contradicting Wiles–Taylor modularity + Ribet.
