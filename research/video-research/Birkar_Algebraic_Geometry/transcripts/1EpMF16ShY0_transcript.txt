I think it's important to have something original something authentic both in
mathematics and also generally in life. Because I believe that a lot of problem
in the world, social and political, arise because people imitate other people,
because countries imitate other countries. It's okay to learn from others,

In algebraic geometry, geometric spaces are called algebraic varieties and some
of them are more special than others. Especially there are some of them which
are called final varieties and the one of the building blocks of algebraic
varieties and what I proved is that if you put some mild conditions on this
kind of varieties then you can parameterize them by only finitely many
set of parameters. In a simple language that just means that you need
only a fixed set of variables and equations of a bounded degree to define
all such varieties.
