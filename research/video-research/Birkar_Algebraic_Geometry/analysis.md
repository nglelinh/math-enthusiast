## 1. Topic status

Major MMP advances and Fano boundedness theorems **proved**. Higher-dimensional classification remains deep and open-ended.

## 2. Key definitions

### Birational equivalence

Varieties isomorphic after removing lower-dimensional sets (rational maps with inverse).

### Minimal model program (MMP)

Algorithm to simplify a variety by contractions and flips toward a minimal or Mori fiber space model.

### Fano variety

Variety with ample anticanonical class — “positively curved” in the algebraic sense.

### Boundedness

Fano varieties of given dimension form a bounded family (finitely many deformation types / controlled equations).


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)
