## 1. Topic status

Several flagship combinatorial conjectures **proved** via algebro-geometric methods. Interface remains fertile.

## 2. Key definitions

### Matroid

Abstract independence structure generalizing linear independence of vectors.

### Characteristic polynomial / chromatic invariants

Combinatorial polynomials with log-concave coefficient conjectures.

### Combinatorial Hodge theory

Cohomology-like packages for combinatorial objects enabling hard Lefschetz / Hodge–Riemann relations.


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)

- Huh (with collaborators Adiprasito, Katz, …): log-concavity and Hodge–Riemann relations in combinatorial settings; Dowling–Wilson for geometric lattices, etc.
