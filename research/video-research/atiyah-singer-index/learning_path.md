# Learning path — Atiyah–Singer (Abel 2004)

## Stage 0 — Orientation (1–2 h)

- Read Abel 2004 popular / citation pages.  
- Watch Abel Prize Interview 2004 (Atiyah & Singer).  
- **Goal:** explain why the prize is about a *program of bridges*, not a 2004 proof date.

## Stage 1 — Slogan theorem (2–4 h)

- Read course lesson EN/VI §1–3.  
- Watch Freed *or* Raghunathan overview (one full pass).  
- **Goal:** write analytic index formula + one special-case name without notes.

## Stage 2 — Foundations (optional, multi-session)

- Khalkhali Index Theory Lec 1 + Lec 18.  
- **Goal:** distinguish K-theory/cobordism culture vs heat-kernel culture at headline level.

## Stage 3 — Seminar product

- ≤400-word memo: “Index theory vs Uhlenbeck-type gauge analysis — two infrastructure layers.”  
- List three confusions you almost made, with corrections.

## Success criteria

- No claim that Abel *proved* the theorem in 2004.  
- Index formula correct; special cases not empty name-drop.  
- Physics clause of citation handled carefully (dialogue ≠ rewrite history).
