## 2. Key definitions

### Simply connected

Every loop can be continuously contracted to a point.

### Ricci flow (slogan)

Metric evolves by $$\partial_t g_{ij}=-2R_{ij}$$, smoothing geometry like heat flow.


---

## 3. Formal status / theorem (seminar honesty)

**Status:** **Proved** (Perelman 2002–2003, Ricci flow with surgery completing Hamilton's program). Only solved Clay Millennium Problem as of 2026.

**Statement / slogan:**

Every simply connected closed 3-manifold is homeomorphic to $$S^3$$. Stronger: Thurston geometrization (Perelman). Method: Ricci flow $$\partial_t g=-2\operatorname{Ric}$$ with surgery at singularities + entropy/monotonicity controls.
