## 1. Topic status

Perfectoid technology is established and widely used; many applications open.

## 2. Key definitions

### Perfectoid field

A complete nonarchimedean field of residue characteristic $$p$$ with Frobenius surjective on $$\mathcal{O}/p$$ (slogan).

### Tilt

Correspondence relating a perfectoid field of char 0 to a perfectoid field of char $$p$$.

### Perfectoid space

Adic spaces locally modeled on perfectoid algebras — bridge for almost mathematics and cohomology.


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)

- Scholze: theory of perfectoid spaces; applications to weight-monodromy, Galois representations, $$p$$-adic Hodge theory.

## 4. Common confusions

| Claim | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| “Perfectoids replace schemes.” | They are a new toolkit for $$p$$-adic / mixed-characteristic geometry, not a replacement of classical schemes. |
