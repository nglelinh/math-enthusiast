## 2. Key definitions (course-aligned)

### Möbius strip

Non-orientable surface with one side and one boundary component.

### Space-filling curve

Continuous surjection $$[0,1]\to[0,1]^2$$ (Peano, Hilbert).

### Banach–Tarski

Ball finite-equidecomposable to two balls via AC; non-measurable pieces.

## 3. Extracted slogans / takeaways

- Local geometric normalcy can fail globally (one-sided surfaces; wild embeddings).
