## 2. Key definitions (course-aligned)

### Sensitive dependence

Nearby initial conditions separate to macroscopic distance.

### Logistic map

$$x_{n+1}=r x_n(1-x_n)$$ on $$[0,1]$$.

### Lyapunov exponent

Average log expansion rate along an orbit.

### Strange attractor

Attracting set with sensitive dynamics; often fractal geometry.
