## 1. Topic status

Foundational advances in making virtual classes rigorous; active applications (mirror symmetry consumers, etc.).

## 2. Key definitions

### Symplectic manifold

Even-dimensional manifold with closed nondegenerate 2-form $$\omega$$.

### J-holomorphic curve

Maps from Riemann surfaces satisfying Cauchy–Riemann equation for an almost complex structure compatible with $$\omega$$.

### Virtual fundamental class

Replacement for fundamental class when moduli spaces are not smooth orbifolds of expected dimension.

### Fukaya category

A∞-category of Lagrangians with morphisms from Floer theory (slogan).
