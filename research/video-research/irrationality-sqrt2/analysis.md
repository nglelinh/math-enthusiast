## 2. Key definitions

### Rational

$$x=a/b$$ with $$a,b\in\mathbb{Z}$$, $$b\neq 0$$.

### Infinite descent (idea)

From a positive integer solution produce a strictly smaller one — impossible.


---

## 3. Formal status / theorem (seminar honesty)

**Status:** **Proved** (classical Pythagorean / Euclidean tradition). Many independent proofs (parity, descent, unique factorization, geometric Apostol).

**Statement / slogan:**

$$\sqrt{2}$$ is irrational: if $$a/b$$ in lowest terms with $$a^2=2b^2$$, then $$a$$ and $$b$$ are both even — contradiction.
