# Mode B — analysis: Navier–Stokes Millennium problem

## Status (2026)

**Open.** Global smooth solutions vs finite-time blow-up for 3D incompressible NS with smooth finite-energy data.

## Key equations

$$
\partial_t u + (u\cdot\nabla)u = \nu\Delta u - \nabla p + f,\qquad \nabla\cdot u=0.
$$

## Known vs open (slogans)

| Result | Status |
|--------|--------|
| Short-time smooth existence | Known |
| 2D global regularity | Known |
| Leray weak solutions (energy class) | Known existence; uniqueness open in 3D |
| Small-data global regularity | Known in various spaces |
| Beale–Kato–Majda vorticity criterion | Conditional regularity |
| Caffarelli–Kohn–Nirenberg partial regularity | Singular set constrained |
| Full 3D large-data global regularity or blow-up | **Open** (Clay) |

## LO6 hygiene

CFD / DNS ≠ continuum theorem. Simulations do not award the Millennium prize. Planes fly without Clay certificates.

## Extraction slogans

1. Viscosity smooths; nonlinear advection and 3D vortex stretching fight smoothness.  
2. Weak ≠ classical.  
3. Kinetic derivations (Hilbert 6th; Deng) are sister problems, not solutions of Clay NS.
