## 1. Topic status

Landmark kinetic derivations (with Hani–Ma) for rarefied hard spheres on timescales of Boltzmann solutions. 3D Navier–Stokes regularity remains **open**.

## 2. Key definitions

### Boltzmann equation

Kinetic PDE for particle density in phase space under collision operators.

### Hard-sphere dynamics

Newtonian particles as elastic hard balls.

### Boltzmann–Grad limit

Low-density scaling connecting particle systems to Boltzmann kinetics.

### Hilbert’s sixth problem (kinetic strand)

Derive continuum / kinetic equations rigorously from microscopic laws.


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)

- Deng–Hani–Ma: long-time derivation of Boltzmann from hard spheres while Boltzmann solution remains regular (arXiv:2408.07818).
- Related: derivation of fluid equations via kinetic theory in stated regimes (arXiv:2503.01800).
