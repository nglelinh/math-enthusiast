## Definitions (slogans)

- **Kakeya / Besicovitch set** in $$\mathbb{R}^n$$: contains a unit line segment in every direction.
- **Conjecture (set form):** such a set must have Hausdorff (and Minkowski) dimension $$n$$.
- Discretized form: unions of $$\delta$$-tubes in separated directions have volume $$\gtrsim_\varepsilon \delta^\varepsilon$$ (up to logs / shadings).

## Extraction slogans

1. Measure zero is compatible with full dimension.  
2. 3D is settled for the set conjecture; high dimensions remain.  
3. Sticky + grains + induction on scales is the research culture slogan—not a student proof.  
4. Fourier restriction / Bochner–Riesz tower is *why analysts care*, not the definition of Kakeya.
