## 2. Key definitions (course-aligned)

### Zeno (dichotomy)

Infinite halves before arrival; series resolution $$\\sum 2^{-n}<\\infty$$.

### Russell

$$R=\{x:x\\notin x\}$$ leads to $$R\\in R\\iff R\\notin R$$ in naive comprehension.

### Semantic paradox

Truth/self-reference puzzles (liar); hierarchy or non-classical fixes.
