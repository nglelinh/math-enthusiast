## 1. Topic status

Major theorems proved (volumes, counting simple geodesics, Eskin–Mirzakhani orbit closures). Field remains active.

## 2. Key definitions

### Moduli space $$\mathcal{M}_g$$

Space of hyperbolic (or complex) structures on a genus-$$g$$ surface up to isomorphism.

### Teichmüller space

Marked surfaces; covers moduli by the mapping class group action.

### Simple closed geodesic

Closed geodesic without self-intersections.

### Earthquake flow

Deformation of hyperbolic structures along measured laminations (Thurston).


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)

- Mirzakhani: recursive formulas for Weil–Petersson volumes; asymptotics for simple closed geodesics.
- Eskin–Mirzakhani (magic wand): orbit closures of $$\mathrm{GL}_2^+$$ actions on moduli of flat surfaces are algebraic / highly structured.
