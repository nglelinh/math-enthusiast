## 1. Topic status

Optimal packing known in dimensions 1, 2, 3, 8, 24. Open in other dimensions.

## 2. Key definitions

### Sphere packing density

Proportion of space occupied by non-overlapping equal balls.

### E8 lattice

Exceptional root lattice in dimension 8; highly symmetric packing.

### Leech lattice

Exceptional lattice in dimension 24.

### Magic function / linear programming bound

Radial Fourier-analytic auxiliary function certifying upper density bounds (Cohn–Elkies style).


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)

- Viazovska: E8 achieves optimal packing density in $$\mathbb{R}^8$$.
- Cohn–Kumar–Miller–Radchenko–Viazovska: Leech optimal in $$\mathbb{R}^{24}$$.

## 4. Common confusions

| Claim | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| “All dimensions solved.” | Only special dimensions; Kepler (3D) was a long separate story. |
| “23-page proof trivial.” | Short after the right modular form is found; decades of Fourier-analytic preparation. |
