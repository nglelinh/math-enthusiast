## 1. Topic status

Poincaré conjecture in dimension 3: **proved** (Perelman 2002–03; community verification). Geometrization proved in the same program.

## 2. Key definitions

### Poincaré conjecture (3D)

Every closed simply connected 3-manifold is homeomorphic to $$S^3$$.

### Ricci flow

$$\partial_t g = -2\mathrm{Ric}(g)$$ — a parabolic deformation of Riemannian metrics.

### Surgery

Cutting and capping necks near singularities so the flow can continue.

### Geometrization

Thurston’s program decomposing 3-manifolds into geometric pieces; implies Poincaré.


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)

- Hamilton: Ricci flow for positive Ricci curvature (1982 program start).
- Perelman: entropy formula; Ricci flow with surgery; finite extinction → Poincaré / geometrization.

## 4. Common confusions

| Claim | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| “Perelman invented Ricci flow.” | Hamilton introduced the flow; Perelman completed the singularity/surgery program. |
| “Clay Prize accepted.” | Perelman declined both Fields (2006) and Clay Millennium Prize (2010). |
