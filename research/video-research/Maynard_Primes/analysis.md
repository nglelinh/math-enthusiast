## 1. Topic status

Bounded gaps **proved** (Zhang 2013; Maynard independent improvement). Twin primes still **open**.

## 2. Key definitions

### Bounded gaps

$$\liminf (p_{n+1}-p_n) < \infty$$.

### Admissible tuple

Integer offsets $$h_1,\ldots,h_k$$ not covering all residues mod any prime.

### GPY sieve / Maynard weights

Selberg-type weights detecting several almost-primes in a tuple.


## 3. Formal results (slogan level)

- Zhang: some finite bound on liminf gaps (70 million initially).
- Maynard: multidimensional weights → better bounds; m primes in bounded length for large admissible tuples.
- Polymath / Maynard: liminf gap ≤ 246 (unconditional improvements over history).

## 4. Common confusions

| Claim | Correction |
|-------|------------|
| “Bounded gaps = twin primes.” | Bound may be 246 or 600; twin primes need gap 2 infinitely often. |
| “Maynard alone.” | Zhang’s breakthrough and GPY foundation are essential history. |
