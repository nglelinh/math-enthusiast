## Core slogan

A-model (symplectic / Fukaya) of $$X$$ computes the same categorical / enumerative data as B-model (complex / coherent sheaves) of a mirror $$\check X$$. SYZ: often dual special-Lagrangian torus fibrations. HMS (Kontsevich): derived category equivalence.
