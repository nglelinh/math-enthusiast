## Definitions to freeze

- **Calabi–Yau (slogan):** complex manifold with trivial canonical bundle (and usually Ricci-flat Kähler metric in physics).
- **A-model:** symplectic / holomorphic curve counts; Fukaya category of Lagrangians.
- **B-model:** complex structure / coherent sheaves; derived category $$D^b\mathrm{Coh}$$.
- **HMS (Kontsevich):** $$D^\pi\mathrm{Fuk}(X)\simeq D^b\mathrm{Coh}(\check X)$$ (precise variants abound).
- **SYZ:** mirror partners as dual special-Lagrangian $$T^n$$-fibrations (T-duality geometricized).

## Confusions

- Mirror ≠ T-duality always (SYZ suggests mechanism; HMS is categorical).
- Not every CY has a unique known mirror construction for students to compute by hand.
- Physics origin ≠ physics proves all math statements.

## Gaps for students

- Floer theory prerequisites for Fukaya categories.
- Algebraic geometry prerequisites for derived categories.
- Do not extract full proofs from popular videos.
