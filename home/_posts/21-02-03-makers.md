### About this collection

Math Enthusiast is a bilingual (English / Vietnamese) collection of essays on modern mathematics—famous problems, Fields Medal and Abel Prize maps, Turing Award computational mathematics, world-changing applications, beautiful concepts, landmark proofs, future frontiers, and open explorations.

The materials are designed for curious learners who want a map of mathematics beyond the standard school syllabus.
